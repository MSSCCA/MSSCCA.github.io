  • 1944

    The Birth of SCCA

    Since 1944, SCCA has championed one mission: To bring motorsports to the masses of American men and women who are passionate about automobiles, speed and competition.

    SCCA is devoted to participation at whichever level you choose. Driving, marshaling, working on a crew or event administration are just some of the countless ways to indulge your passion with like-minded people from all walks of life.
